Frequently asked questions

Your AI Job Kit includes: an ATS-optimised CV, a tailored cover letter, a 4-email follow-up strategy, a gap analysis report, LinkedIn profile optimization (Pro+), and a 13-question interview prep guide with strong answers (Pro+). All generated from a single job description paste.

Is this only for tech roles?

No. The system works for finance, banking, NGOs, admin, healthcare, education, tech, government, customer service, and more. The AI adapts to whatever job description you paste in — it has specific knowledge of Kenyan salary ranges, sector keywords, and employer expectations across 10 industries.

What if I have little or no work experience?

The AI is designed for fresh graduates and career changers. It turns university projects, industrial attachment, volunteering, and coursework into quantified achievements. Your class projects and side work count — the AI knows how to frame them.

Can I use it for multiple job applications?

Yes. Lifetime access means you generate new CVs and cover letters for every role you apply to — just paste a new job description and regenerate. One payment, unlimited applications, forever.
