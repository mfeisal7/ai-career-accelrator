Frequently asked questions

What exactly do I get?

Your AI Job Kit includes: an ATS-optimised CV, a tailored cover letter, a 4-email follow-up strategy, a gap analysis report, LinkedIn profile optimization (Pro+), and a 13-question interview prep guide with strong answers (Pro+).

Is this only for tech roles?

No. The system works for finance, banking, NGOs, admin, healthcare, education, tech, government, customer service, and more. The AI adapts to whatever job description you paste in.

What if I have no work experience?

We specialise in fresh graduates and career changers. The AI turns university projects, industrial attachment, volunteering, and coursework into quantified achievements that Kenyan hiring managers recognise.

How does payment work?

Click any "Get [Plan]" button above — it opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. We reply with M-Pesa Paybill instructions. You pay (30 seconds), we confirm, and your access unlocks within 15 minutes. Return to the app and click "refresh my access."

How does the guarantee work?

Use the job kit as instructed, apply to at least 15 appropriate roles, and track your applications for 45 days. If you don't get at least 3 interviews, send us proof and we refund your payment in full via M-Pesa.

Can I use it for multiple job applications?

Yes. Lifetime access means you can generate new CVs and cover letters for every role you apply to. Just paste a new job description and re-generate. Your account is yours forever.
